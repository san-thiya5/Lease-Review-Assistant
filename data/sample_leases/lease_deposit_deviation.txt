RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

1. Parties and Premises
This Residential Lease Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of January 15, 2026, by and between Apex Property Holdings LLC ("Landlord") and Jane Doe ("Tenant"). Landlord agrees to lease to Tenant, and Tenant agrees to lease from Landlord, the residential premises located at 742 Evergreen Terrace, Unit 4B, Springfield, IL ("Premises"), for residential dwelling use only.

2. Lease Term
The term of this Lease shall commence on February 1, 2026, and shall terminate on January 31, 2027 ("Term"), unless renewed or terminated earlier in accordance with the express provisions of this Agreement.

3. Rent and Late Fees
Tenant agrees to pay Landlord a base monthly rent of $2,500.00, payable on or before the first day of each calendar month. In the event rent is received more than five (5) days past due, Tenant shall pay a one-time late fee of $75.00 (representing 3.0% of the monthly rent). No compounding or daily late charges shall apply.

4. Security Deposit
Upon execution of this Agreement, Tenant shall deposit with Landlord the sum of $8,750.00, representing three and one-half (3.5) months' base rent, as a security deposit for the faithful performance of Tenant's covenants herein.

5. Return of Deposit Timeline
Within twenty-one (21) calendar days after Tenant has vacated the Premises, surrendered all keys, and terminated tenancy, Landlord shall refund the full security deposit to Tenant, less any lawful deductions for unpaid rent or actual repairs beyond reasonable wear and tear. Any deductions will be accompanied by an itemized written statement delivered to Tenant's forwarding address.

6. Maintenance Responsibility
Landlord shall be responsible for maintaining the structural components of the building, including the roof, exterior walls, foundation, and all shared building facilities, as well as maintaining in good working order the primary mechanical, heating, electrical, and plumbing systems. Tenant shall keep the interior of the Premises clean, sanitary, and safe, perform routine minor interior care, and promptly notify Landlord in writing of any defects or needed repairs.

7. Subletting and Assignment
Tenant may not assign this Lease or sublet any portion of the Premises without obtaining the prior written consent of Landlord. Landlord agrees that such consent shall not be unreasonably withheld, delayed, or conditioned upon onerous terms.

8. Renewal Terms
Either party may propose to renew this Lease by delivering written notice to the other party at least forty-five (45) calendar days prior to the expiration of the current Term. Renewal shall be subject to mutual written agreement between the parties on prevailing market rental rates and shall not lock in automatic multi-year commitments without explicit written consent.

9. Notice Period for Termination
Either party may terminate this tenancy at the conclusion of the initial Term or any agreed month-to-month extension by providing the other party with a minimum of forty-five (45) days advance written notice.

10. Default and Due Process
If Tenant fails to pay rent when due or breaches any material covenant, Landlord shall provide written notice of default specifying a statutory cure period. In no event shall Landlord exercise self-help remedies, lockouts, or seizure of personal property without adhering to statutory notices and formal judicial eviction proceedings.
